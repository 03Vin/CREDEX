# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

## Target User
The exact target user is a **SaaS Startup Founder** or a **Lead Engineering Manager** at a Seed or Series A stage company (team size 5–50). 
- They are burning through cash and looking for ways to extend their runway.
- They have a team of developers using AI tools daily but haven't audited the costs because they are focused on shipping features.
- They are likely the person who approves the company credit card bills and sighs at the growing line items for Cursor, Claude, and OpenAI.

## What They Search or Scroll For
Right before they would want this tool, they are likely searching for:
- "How to reduce AI API costs"
- "Cursor vs GitHub Copilot pricing comparison"
- "Is Claude Team plan worth it?"
- "Startups overspending on AI"
- "How to optimize OpenAI API usage"

They are scrolling through Twitter threads about "AI bill shock" or reading Hacker News posts about "How we saved $X by switching models."

## Where They Hang Out Online
- **Communities**: Indie Hackers, Y Combinator founder Slack groups, specific Discord servers for AI developers (like Latent Space or LangChain).
- **Subreddits**: r/startups, r/saas, r/SideProject.
- **Social**: X (Twitter) lists of "Build in Public" founders and tech leads.
- **Newsletters**: Ben's Bites, TLDR, or other tech newsletters that curate tools.

## How to Get the First 100 Users in 30 Days ($0 Budget)
1.  **Hacker News Launch**: Write a show HN post titled: "Show HN: I built a free tool to audit your startup's AI spend." Focus on the problem of "invisible spend" and offer value without a paywall.
2.  **Direct Outreach**: Find 20 founders on X who post about their startup's tech stack or complaining about costs. Send them a personalized DM: "Hey, I saw you use Cursor and Claude. I built a free tool to see if you're on the right plan. It takes 1 min. Here's the link: [URL]."
3.  **Indie Hackers Post**: Write a detailed post on Indie Hackers sharing the data from the first few audits (anonymized) to show the value.
4.  **Slack/Discord Drop**: Share it in "self-promotion" channels of startup communities with a clear value proposition: "Save money on your AI stack."

## The Unfair Distribution Channel
The unfair channel is **Credex's own network**. Since Credex already sells discounted credits, they likely have a list of companies that didn't buy because they didn't think they needed them. Sending this tool to *lost leads* as a "free value add" can reactivate them. If the tool shows they *are* overspending, they become active buyers for Credex credits.

## Week-1 Traction
If this works, week-1 traction looks like:
- **Audits Completed**: 150+
- **Emails Captured**: 80+ (assuming ~50% conversion if they see value)
- **Credex Leads Generated**: 15+ (companies showing >$500/mo savings)
- **Social Shares**: 10+ shares of the results page on X.
