# Economics

## Converted Lead Value to Credex
Let's assume a converted lead is a startup that decides to purchase AI infrastructure credits through Credex instead of paying retail.
- **Average Spend**: A typical Seed stage startup might spend $1,000/month on AI tools (Cursor, Claude, OpenAI APIs).
- **Credex Discount**: Credex offers a discount (e.g., 20%).
- **Credex Margin**: Credex makes a margin on the credits sold (e.g., 10% of the spend).
- **Revenue per Lead**: 10% of $1,000/mo = $100/mo per converted lead.
- **Annual Value**: $1,200/year in net revenue to Credex per lead.

## Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
Since the GTM plan relies on organic channels ($0 budget), the direct CAC is $0. However, accounting for time and effort:
- **Direct Outreach**: 100 DMs takes ~3 hours. If 10 convert to audits, and 1 to a sale, the time cost is ~3 hours of work.
- **Hacker News**: Writing a post takes 1 hour. If it yields 100 audits and 2 sales, it's highly efficient.
- **Blended CAC**: Effectively under $20 in time value per acquired customer, making it extremely efficient.

## Conversion Rates for Profitability
To be profitable (assuming the tool costs $100/mo to run for hosting and API fees):
- We need at least 1 conversion per month to cover costs ($100/mo revenue).
- If the conversion rate from **Audit** -> **Lead** is 10%, and **Lead** -> **Sale** is 10%:
- We need 100 audits per month to get 10 leads, yielding 1 sale.
- This is a very achievable target.

## Path to $1M ARR in 18 Months
To drive $1,000,000 in Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) in 18 months:
- We need $83,333 in monthly revenue.
- At $100/mo margin per converted lead, we need **833 active converted leads**.
- To get 833 conversions in 18 months (~46 per month):
- Assuming a blended conversion rate of 1% from audit to sale (10% audit -> lead, 10% lead -> sale):
- We need **4,600 audits per month** or ~150 audits per day.

**What must be true for this to happen**:
1.  **Virality**: The tool must go viral on Twitter or Hacker News frequently, or the embeddable widget must be adopted by high-traffic sites.
2.  **Broader Tool Support**: We must support enterprise tools with higher spends (e.g., dedicated capacity) where the margin per lead is much higher (e.g., $1,000/mo margin instead of $100/mo). If 10% of leads are enterprise, the number of required audits drops significantly.
3.  **Partnerships**: Credex partners with startup accelerators (like YC) to make this tool the standard for audit before demo day.
