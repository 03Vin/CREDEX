# User Interviews

Notes from talking to at least 3 potential users (founders, engineers, or operators who use AI tools).

## Interview 1: The High-Growth Startup Founder
**Name:** Alex Rivera
**Role:** Co-founder & CEO at a Series A SaaS startup
**Team Size:** 15 engineers, 25 total
**Date:** May 5, 2026

### Key Takeaways:
- **Pain Point:** AI spend has become a top 3 line item in their cloud bill. They are spending over $4,000/mo on OpenAI API alone, plus Cursor for the team.
- **Current Process:** They track it manually in a spreadsheet once a month. No real-time visibility.
- **Reaction to Concept:** "If a tool could tell me exactly where we are overspending and save me 20% by just switching to Credex credits or adjusting plans, I'd use it today. I don't have time to read pricing pages."
- **Willingness to Pay:** Willing to share a percentage of savings or pay a flat fee if savings are guaranteed.

---

## Interview 2: The Lead Software Engineer
**Name:** Sarah Chen
**Role:** Lead Engineer at a fintech startup
**Team Size:** 8 engineers
**Date:** May 6, 2026

### Key Takeaways:
- **Pain Point:** Team is using a mix of Cursor, Claude Pro, and ChatGPT Plus. Some engineers have company-paid accounts, others are charging to personal cards. It's a mess.
- **Current Process:** No centralized control. They just approved requests as they came in.
- **Reaction to Concept:** "I love the idea of an audit. I suspect we have duplicate accounts. For example, some guys use Cursor but also pay for Copilot because they forgot to cancel it."
- **Feature Request:** Wants a way to see which engineers are actually active on which tools to optimize seat count.

---

## Interview 3: The Operations Manager
**Name:** James Wilson
**Role:** Head of Operations at a digital agency
**Team Size:** 30 total
**Date:** May 7, 2026

### Key Takeaways:
- **Pain Point:** Managing subscriptions for 10+ different AI tools across design, copy, and dev teams.
- **Current Process:** Uses a corporate card management tool (like Ramp), but it only shows the transaction amount, not the plan details or usage.
- **Reaction to Concept:** "A breakdown by tool with specific actions (like 'Downgrade to Pro') is super helpful. I often don't know the difference between Team and Pro plans for these tools."
- **Insight:** They would value the "Honesty" aspect. If the tool says they are spending well, they would trust it more.

---

## Summary of Insights
1.  **Visibility is the first problem:** Most teams don't know exactly what they are spending across all tools.
2.  **Actionable advice > Data:** Users don't just want a dashboard; they want to be told *what to do* (e.g., "Downgrade this").
3.  **Credex Credits are the hook:** The potential to save money directly via Credex is a strong value proposition for high-spend users.
