## FAQ Section (5 Q&As)

### 1. Is this tool really free?
Yes, the audit is 100% free. We generate revenue by helping companies with significant savings opportunities purchase credits through Credex at a discount.

### 2. Do I need to connect my bank or API keys?
No. This is a manual input audit. You just tell us what tools you use and what you pay, and we give you the optimization plan. We never ask for sensitive credentials.

### 3. How accurate is the audit logic?
The logic is based on the official pricing and plan limits of the major AI vendors (Cursor, Copilot, Claude, etc.) as of the current week. It is highly accurate for standard usage patterns.

### 4. What happens to my data?
We respect your privacy. If you share your results via the public URL, identifying details like your email and company name are stripped out. Only the tools and savings are shown.

### 5. How does Credex save me money?
Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits sourced from companies that overforecasted or pivoted. If the audit shows you can save money, we can help you get those credits.
